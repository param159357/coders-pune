CampusConnect - Meeting Notes
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA. This describes a fictional project and no real institution.

Meeting 1 - Scope review

The team selected option A for the first release. Option A delivers event discovery and
registration only. Option B would have added payment handling in the same release.

The reason given for selecting option A was that the payment provider contract had not been
signed, and the team did not want the release date to depend on a contract outside its
control.

Payment integration was postponed until after the first release as a direct result of that
decision.

Meeting 2 - Registration design

It was agreed that registration will capture the student identifier, the event identifier,
and a timestamp. Registration will not capture dietary requirements in the first release,
because storing that data was judged to need a privacy review that has not been scheduled.

The team agreed that the maximum capacity for a single event is 500 attendees.

Meeting 3 - Release readiness

The release depends on approval of the final event data format by the timetable team. That
approval has been requested and has not yet been given.

A launch date was discussed but not agreed. No confirmed launch date is documented.

Action items recorded in this meeting were to finish registration validation and to arrange
a test email service. No owner was recorded against either action.
