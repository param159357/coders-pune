CampusConnect - Project Brief
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA. This describes a fictional project and no real institution.

Purpose

CampusConnect is a campus event and student-services portal. It gives students a single
place to find out what is happening on campus and to register for it, and gives student
societies a simple way to publish their events.

Scope of the first release

The initial release focuses on event discovery and student registration. Students can
browse a list of upcoming events, filter them by category and date, open an event to see
its details, and register for a place.

Payment integration was postponed until after the first release. Events that require a fee
are listed with the fee shown, but payment is collected offline by the organising society.

Out of scope for the first release

Society self-service account creation, the alumni directory, and the mobile application
are all out of scope for the first release. They were discussed but deliberately deferred.

Users

The portal serves three groups: students who discover and register for events, society
organisers who publish and manage events, and student-services staff who moderate
published content.

Constraints

The portal must meet the institution's accessibility standard before it can be published
to students. Event data is supplied by the existing campus timetable system, so the format
of that data is agreed with the timetable team rather than chosen by this project.
