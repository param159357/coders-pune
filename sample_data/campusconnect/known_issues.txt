CampusConnect - Known Issues, Risks and Open Questions
SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA. This describes a fictional project and no real institution.

Blockers

Email notification testing is blocked by the absence of a configured test email service.
Registration confirmation emails cannot be verified until that service exists, so the
notification code is written but unverified.

Dependencies

The release depends on approval of the final event data format. Until the timetable team
approves the format, the integration cannot be treated as stable.

The accessibility review depends on the institution's accessibility team, who have not yet
been formally approached.

Risks

If the event data format changes after development is complete, the timetable integration
will need rework, and that rework would delay the release.

There is a risk that the moderation queue is not finished before the release, which would
leave published content unmoderated.

Open questions

It is unclear whether students should be able to cancel a registration themselves, or
whether cancellation must go through a society organiser. This has not been decided.

The owner for the final accessibility review is not documented.

No confirmed launch date is documented.
